Plate01

Kerala Crop Price Forecaster

Live service · Telegram bot · Malayalam

Same day, same banana, seven Kerala markets: ₹51/kg in one, ₹70/kg in another. The prices are published daily on Agmarknet — in English, hard to search, with no outlook and no alerts — so most farmers go by what the middleman tells them. This bot does the checking for them: today's price in every market for 17 crops, the range to expect over the coming week, and a message when a crop crosses the price you named.

What the data said A model that loses to the obvious guess is still a result. Reporting it, and shipping the baseline instead, is the difference between a forecast a farmer can trust and one that just looks sophisticated.

Plate02

Agentic Data Intelligence Platform

PG Diploma capstone · nine agents

Nine agents carry raw business data from upload to decision: ingesting CSV, Excel, SQL and REST behind one data contract, learning what normal looks like, then catching schema drift, null floods and distribution shifts in every new file. The architecture's whole point is that the model never holds authority — it proposes, and a deterministic gate derived from the firing rule decides what may actually run.

What the data said I logged the model's self-reported confidence across live runs. 82% came back at exactly 0.95, inside a band 0.08 wide. The confidence threshold was doing no routing work at all; the deterministic layers had been carrying the system the whole time.

Plate03

Kerala Flood Risk Intelligence

Property-level risk from terrain

Answers one question: if I buy this property in Kerala, will it flood? Not by predicting rain, but by reading terrain — elevation, slope, distance to water, catchment area and historical paddy land, drawn from Copernicus DEM, Sentinel-1, Dynamic World and 159,168 OpenStreetMap waterway features. A Random Forest scores it, twelve physics-based rules constrain it where pure ML fails on geographic edge cases, and SHAP explains each result.

Bilingual retrieval · Malayalam & English

Ask in Malayalam or English which government schemes you qualify for. Method

Measure before optimising

A chain taking 28 seconds turned out to be spending 2.3 of them per call on a DNS fallback, not on the model. Profiling the transport beat tuning the part everyone assumes is slow.

Falsify your own guarantees

Every safety claim in the platform has a script that removes the protection and proves the test catches it. A guarantee nobody has tried to break is a guess.

Report what failed

The model that lost to a naive baseline. The confidence score that meant nothing. The passing test that asserted nothing. They go in the write-up too — they are what tells you the rest is true.
